# Inner Keep surface contract

Mode: Experience. This extends the established Invisible Castle pixel-art game.
The user's supplied second-level castle layout, blue masonry, amber wood, blue and gold
heraldry, green exit, white-haired protagonist and uniformed guard define the visual direction.
Assumptions were explicitly delegated by the user; no interview was requested.

First viewport: immediately playable north cell, recognizably furnished using complete newly
generated sprites. Compact pixel HUD and real empty inventory remain outside the world.
The character must stay legible at the close gameplay zoom, with the camera following
movement and respecting the world bounds at resized windows.

Exploration: thirteen connected rooms. Physical solids must match furnishings and walls,
with roomy passages. Room plaques and NPC hints provide local wayfinding. Colored floor
materials distinguish rooms while remaining inside the incumbent palette.

Signature interaction: searching and crafting turn the hero toward the object and show
directional reaching with cancellable progress. Control-room exposure drives a visible
warning. The utility puzzle labels and colors each terminal; mistakes are recoverable.
The green exit retracts only after power repair and cutting the chain.

Scope: editable native scene and assets, first-chapter continuation, portable generic camera
viewport inset and configurable completion copy. Retain existing project identity, bitmap
font, square HUD bands and inventory pattern. No web detector applies to this SDL surface.
Artwork provenance: eight built-in image-generation masters and exact prompt files, with
reference files used only for style/layout. Native plaques are exact-text bitmap UI.
